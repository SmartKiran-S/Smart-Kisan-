SEASONAL PLANNING

A little planning goes a long way

A compact seasonal checklist rather than a complicated calendar. Adjust activities to your crop, soil and local agricultural guidance.

🗓️
Seasonal field checklist

Before sowing: check soil moisture, seed suitability and the local rainfall outlook. During growth: monitor pests, nutrient needs and drainage. Before harvest: check rain and wind forecasts, then plan safe storage.

🌧️

Before the rain

Clear drainage channels, secure equipment and avoid spraying when rain is likely to wash treatments away.

🌱

During growth

Walk the field regularly, look under leaves for pests, and base irrigation and fertilizer decisions on crop stage.

🌾

Near harvest

Track rain and wind, prepare a dry storage space and follow safe handling practices for your crop.
